# RAND/Ding base-input trace

Ding et al. estimated 2013 costs. T2D per-case costs came from IDF sources. IHD, stroke, breast cancer and colon cancer per-case costs were constructed from EU-27 costs divided by GBD prevalence and extrapolated to China with a health-expenditure-per-capita weight. These are modelled country inputs, not China disease-cost studies.

China's published 3,075,139 thousand INT$ attributable direct cost is retained only as an external model-output reference. Exact China disease-specific per-case inputs require the online appendix (p27; attributable outputs p31), which was not acquired publicly. This item remains open.
