# Annual avoided-incidence cohort engine

The model uses the previous round's 1,664 verified IHD/ischemic-stroke age–sex–year incidence records unchanged. Policy action enters **only** as `incident_cases_averted`. For each annual entry cohort, the engine advances age one year at a time through 2050. Disease death, other-cause death and remission are competing exits from the diseased state. Other-cause death and recurrence are competing exits from the off-disease state. For hazards `h_j`, total exit probability is `1−exp(−Σh_j)`, split by `h_j/Σh_j`. Surviving/remitted/recurrent counts carry to the next year. Annual diseased person-years use midpoint occupancy. YLD = patient-years × disease disability weight; YLL = disease deaths × standard remaining life years; DALY = YLD + YLL.

The engine tracks the *difference* cohort under common transition hazards. This is algebraically a baseline-versus-intervention difference conditional on transition invariance. It does not model historical prevalent disease, nor estimate total disease-state prevalence in either absolute scenario. Annual age groups are indexed by the lower age in the 5-year entry interval; a future scientific run needs Work approval of the within-band age distribution or finer population input. The open 95+ group remains open. Cross-disease competing morbidity is outside this two-disease pilot.

All transition inputs are disease, sex, age and time-since-incidence keyed. Parameters are injected; the engine rejects wrong keys and missing values. The verified 2023 GBD export now supplies age-sex prevalence, deaths, YLD, YLL and DALY Rate/Number data for IHD and ischemic stroke, including 95+. The 640 detailed rows populate calibration or external-validation fields; 160 overlapping 70+/75+/80+/85+ aggregates are retained in the raw ZIP but excluded from model interfaces. Point burden ratios are descriptive. Their UI fields stay blank because joint draws or covariance are unavailable. These GBD rates and ratios are **not** transition probabilities.

The verified all-cause export supplies 32 detailed age-sex Deaths rates with Rate/Number and point/UI. These rates are calibration targets. They are not directly used as annual hazards because the engine requires a documented rate-to-hazard conversion and an other-cause component that avoids double counting target-disease mortality. Disease survival, remission, recurrence, excess mortality, case fatality, disability-state mapping and the standard YLL life table remain missing or unadmitted. Therefore empirical dynamic outcomes remain blocked.

The full 1,664-row incidence input was used in a deterministic software fixture with explicit `SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY` hazards, disability weight and life years. That fixture demonstrates annual loops, competing risks, aging and aggregation. Its output is under `tests/`, is never interpreted epidemiologically and cannot enter the formal run. The actual dynamic results files are blocked with blank numeric fields and `BLOCKED_DYNAMIC_PARAMETERS`. No GBD death, prevalence, YLD, YLL or DALY endpoint is multiplied by an incidence PIF.

The local runner validates all three original GBD ZIP hashes, frozen incidence detail, 640 detailed disease-endpoint rows, 320 detailed all-cause rows, excluded aggregate-age rows, RR interface, Rate/Number population consistency, 48 tests and five notebooks. Scientific parameters require Work review before changing the result gate.
