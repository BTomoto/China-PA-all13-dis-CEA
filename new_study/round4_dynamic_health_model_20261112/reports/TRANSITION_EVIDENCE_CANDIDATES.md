# Disease-course evidence candidates (none admitted)

These are candidate studies to extract in a later disease-specific evidence review. Their published end points cannot be inserted directly as annual transition probabilities.

| Disease | Primary study | Available concept | Transfer limit and next extraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ischemic stroke | [China Stroke Prevention and Control Report 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12795483/) | Nationwide program sample with subtype outcomes | Hospital cases and follow-up windows; extract subtype-specific death, disability, recurrence with denominators, age/sex and horizon. |
| Ischemic stroke | [CNSR registry trends](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8258052/) | One-year vascular death and recurrence by phase | Registry contains AIS/TIA and selected hospitals; determine first-ever versus recurrent index events and time-specific survival. |
| IHD | [China PEACE prospective AMI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7873426/) | Recurrent AMI and one-year mortality | AMI is a subset of GBD IHD; extract all IHD component coverage, age/sex and post-discharge selection before use. |
| Both | [GBD 2023 causes of death methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(25)01917-8) | YLL definition as cause deaths times standard remaining life expectancy | Obtain the actual GBD 2023 standard life table; national all-cause mortality is a separate competing-risk input. |

No study above is admitted. Cohort population, entry disease definition, sampling, event timing, post-event survival, age/sex detail, uncertainty and transportability to national 2025–2050 cohorts remain unresolved. Work must approve a calibration/transport method after multi-source extraction.
